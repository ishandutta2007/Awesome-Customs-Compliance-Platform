# Awesome-Customs-Compliance-Platform

## Top Customs Compliance Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Import/Export Compliance, Tariff Classification, Duty Calculation, Restricted-Party Screening, Filing & Global Trade Management*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Customs Compliance**. These systems help importers and exporters classify goods, calculate duties, screen restricted parties, manage licenses, and file customs declarations across jurisdictions.



**Examples** include Descartes Customs, Thomson Reuters ONESOURCE Global Trade, E2open Global Trade, Mic Customs Solutions, AEB Customs Management, Integration Point, Customs4trade, SAP Global Trade Services, Livingston Trade Management, and TradeWindow (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Production customs and global trade compliance is almost entirely commercial due to regulatory data, filing connectivity, and liability requirements. Meaningful open foundations are limited to experimental platforms, ERPNext EXIM modules, and open sanctions-list tooling. This section expands those while remaining realistic about the commercial gap.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Descartes Customs / CustomsInfo](https://www.descartes.com/)**  

  Deep tariff and customs data platform with classification, duty content, and integration into enterprise trade systems.



- **[Thomson Reuters ONESOURCE Global Trade](https://www.thomsonreuters.com/)**  

  Broad cloud suite for import/export compliance, classification, screening, customs, and landed-cost workflows.



- **[E2open Global Trade](https://www.e2open.com/)**  

  Enterprise global trade management covering customs, compliance, and multi-country filing (Amber Road lineage).



- **[MIC Customs Solutions](https://www.mic-cust.com/)**  

  Customs and trade compliance software for declaration, classification, and European/global customs processes.



- **[AEB Customs Management](https://www.aeb.com/)**  

  Customs and export management solutions for classification, declarations, and trade compliance automation.



- **[Integration Point](https://www.integrationpoint.com/)**  

  Global trade management and customs compliance platform for importers and exporters.



- **[Customs4trade](https://www.customs4trade.com/)**  

  Customs and trade compliance solutions focused on classification, duty, and declaration workflows.



- **[SAP Global Trade Services (GTS)](https://www.sap.com/)**  

  SAP’s trade compliance and customs suite integrated with ERP for screening, classification, and customs processing.



- **[Livingston Trade Management](https://www.livingstonintl.com/)**  

  Trade management and customs brokerage-oriented compliance services and technology.



- **[TradeWindow](https://www.tradewindow.io/)**  

  Digital trade and customs-oriented platform supporting cross-border documentation and compliance workflows.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Experimental open trade compliance platforms](https://github.com/)**  

  Early-stage open efforts aimed at denied-party screening, classification helpers, and licence tracking (not production customs filing systems).



- **[ERPNext EXIM / export-import modules](https://github.com/finbyz/exim)**  

  Open modules extending ERPNext for export-import documentation, benefits claims, and basic compliance workflows.



- **[Open sanctions and restricted-party list tools](https://github.com/)**  

  Community libraries and data pipelines for OFAC, EU, UN, and other public sanctions lists used in screening prototypes.



- **[HTS / tariff code open datasets and helpers](https://github.com/)**  

  Scripts and datasets for working with public Harmonized System and tariff schedule data.



- **[Document and packing-list open generators](https://github.com/)**  

  Templates and tools for commercial invoices, packing lists, and basic trade documents.



- **[AES / filing-related open research tools](https://github.com/)**  

  Limited open utilities around electronic filing standards (not certified production filers).



- **[Compliance checklist and audit open templates](https://github.com/)**  

  Shared checklists for export controls, recordkeeping, and internal trade compliance programs.



- **[Documentation and trade compliance playbooks](https://github.com/)**  

  Open guides on classification principles, screening practices, and process design for practitioners.



- **[Self-hosted screening + ERP patterns](https://github.com/)**  

  Combining open sanctions lists with ERPNext or similar for basic internal screening before commercial systems.



- **[WCO and public trade data open loaders](https://github.com/)**  

  Tools for ingesting publicly available customs and trade statistics for analysis (not operational compliance).



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Using open sanctions-list libraries for internal pre-screening prototypes.

- Extending **ERPNext EXIM** for documentation-heavy export processes in smaller organizations.

- Accepting that certified multi-country filing, maintained tariff databases, restricted-party content, broker connectivity, and audit-grade compliance still require commercial platforms (Descartes, ONESOURCE, E2open, SAP GTS, AEB, MIC, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on transparency of screening logic and reducing manual checklist work for low-volume traders.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Screen against open sanctions data → classify with public HS helpers → generate documents from templates → hand off to a licensed broker or commercial GTM for filing. Suitable for education and very small trade programs. Importers/exporters with material volume almost always run commercial customs compliance platforms.
